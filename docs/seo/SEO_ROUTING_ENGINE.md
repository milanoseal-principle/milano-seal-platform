# SEO Routing Engine

## Core SEO Routing Rules

### Rule 1: One Service + One Geo Maximum
Each page targets a maximum of one primary service and one primary geographic modifier. No page should attempt to rank for multiple services or multiple geos simultaneously.

**Correct:** "Florida apostille for Italy documents"
**Incorrect:** "Florida apostille notary authentication Italy Spain Portugal documents"

### Rule 2: No Keyword Stuffing
- No meta title over 65 characters
- No meta description over 160 characters
- No H1 that contains more than one primary keyword phrase
- No paragraph that repeats the target keyword more than twice
- No hidden or visually obscured keyword lists

### Rule 3: Corridor Page Structure
Each corridor page (/countries/[slug]) targets:
- Primary keyword: "[Country] apostille"
- Secondary keyword: "Florida to [Country] document authentication"
- Geographic modifier: "Florida" as origin
- Do not add additional country names to corridor pages

### Rule 4: Service Page Structure
Each service page (/services/[service]) targets:
- Primary keyword: "[Service name] Florida"
- Secondary keyword: "[Service name] Tampa Bay" or "[Service name] international"
- Do not combine multiple service names in a single title

### Rule 5: Professional Page Structure
Each professional page (/professionals/[segment]) targets:
- Primary keyword: "[Profession] document execution support"
- Do not add geographic modifiers to professional pages unless the page is specifically geo-targeted

---

## URL Structure Rules
- Use hyphens, not underscores
- Keep slugs under 50 characters
- Do not include stop words in slugs
- Use singular nouns for service pages
- Use plural nouns for category pages

## Internal Linking Rules
- Every corridor page links to at least two related service pages
- Every service page links to at least two related corridor pages
- Professional pages link to relevant corridor and service pages
- No orphan pages — every page must be reachable from at least one internal link

## Canonical Rules
- Every page has a canonical tag pointing to itself
- No duplicate content between corridor pages and vertical pages
- Country pages and vertical pages are separate — do not duplicate body copy
