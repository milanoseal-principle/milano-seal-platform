# The Milano Seal™ — RON Alliance Coverage Network

## Purpose

The Milano Seal Alliance is a governed network of commissioned notaries and execution partners that can support eligible U.S. document matters after credential, jurisdiction, Matter, payment, and execution gates are satisfied.

## Partner Classes

1. RON execution partner
2. Mobile / wet-ink fallback partner
3. Specialist execution partner

Specialist capabilities may include airport-area availability, after-hours availability, bilingual support, hospital/facility coverage, witness coordination, scan-back support, apostille familiarity, and executive/private-client service.

## Priority Recruitment States

Florida, Texas, California, New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Illinois, Virginia, Colorado, Arizona, Nevada, Georgia, North Carolina, Ohio, Michigan, Washington, Oregon, Utah, Tennessee, Indiana.

This is a recruitment-priority list only. It is not a legal statement that RON is currently available or appropriate in every listed jurisdiction.

## Verification Levels

- LISTED — application received; not dispatch-ready.
- VERIFIED_NOTARY — current commission and core credentials reviewed.
- RON_READY — RON authorization/registration and platform capability reviewed; Matter dispatch still requires state and receiving-authority review.
- EXECUTION_PARTNER — operator-approved and SOP-ready.
- PRIORITY_DISPATCH_PARTNER — trusted for urgent/high-control execution when the Matter independently qualifies.

## Dispatch Boundary

Partner readiness never authorizes execution by itself.

A RON dispatch must remain blocked or in review unless all relevant controls are satisfied, including:

- current signer-state authority review;
- partner commission and RON authorization/registration review;
- receiving-authority posture;
- wet-ink/original requirements;
- canonical Matter execution gate;
- verified Wise payment gate when payment is required;
- operator dispatch approval.

## Public Claim Rules

Use:

- state-authorized RON notary;
- RON-capable notary;
- Remote Online Notarization-capable partner;
- approved/registered online notary where required by state.

Do not use:

- federally certified RON;
- nationally certified RON;
- TSA certified;
- guaranteed foreign acceptance;
- guaranteed dispatch volume;
- guaranteed income;
- guaranteed assignments.
