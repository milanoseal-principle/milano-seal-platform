# RON Partner Verification SOP

## Intake

Capture:

- full legal name;
- business name when applicable;
- state commission;
- commission number and expiration;
- RON authorization/registration assertion;
- RON platform(s);
- E&O information;
- background-check posture;
- languages;
- mobile / wet-ink availability;
- after-hours / weekend / airport-area availability;
- preferred service area;
- international-document and apostille experience;
- shipping / scan-back capability;
- document classes accepted and declined;
- W-9 readiness;
- NDA willingness;
- Milano SOP training willingness.

## Review Sequence

1. Confirm commission identity and expiration.
2. Confirm state-specific RON authorization or registration requirements from an official current source before marking RON-ready.
3. Confirm platform capability and any required digital certificate/eSeal posture.
4. Review E&O information and required state bond/credential evidence.
5. Review availability and service boundaries.
6. Review data-handling and confidentiality requirements.
7. Complete Milano SOP training.
8. Complete a controlled test or first-Matter review before priority dispatch status.

## Levels

### LISTED
Application received. No dispatch.

### VERIFIED_NOTARY
Current commission/core credentials reviewed. RON not yet treated as available unless separately verified.

### RON_READY
State authorization/registration and platform capability reviewed. Still subject to Matter-level receiving-authority and execution review.

### EXECUTION_PARTNER
SOP-ready and operator-approved.

### PRIORITY_DISPATCH_PARTNER
Operator-approved for high-control or urgent assignments when the Matter independently qualifies.

## Fail-Closed Rules

- Missing or expired commission → BLOCKED.
- Unknown current state RON authority → REVIEW_REQUIRED.
- Unknown receiving-authority RON acceptance → REVIEW_REQUIRED.
- Wet-ink/original required → RON BLOCKED for that Matter.
- Payment not verified when payment is required → dispatch remains blocked.
- Partner readiness never changes canonical Matter state.

## Reverification

Re-review at minimum when:

- commission renews or expires;
- RON registration changes;
- platform changes;
- E&O expires/changes;
- partner changes state coverage;
- a compliance incident occurs;
- state law/platform rules materially change.
