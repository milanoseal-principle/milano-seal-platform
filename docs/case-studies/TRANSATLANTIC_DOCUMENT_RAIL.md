# The Transatlantic Document Rail

**Case type:** International citizenship document execution and apostille coordination

A U.S.-based client required a multi-state document pathway for Italian
citizenship recognition. Records originated in multiple U.S. jurisdictions and
required Italian receiving-authority review.

## Routing Principle

A competent authority can authenticate only signatures or public seals within
its jurisdiction. Florida documents require Florida authority review, Colorado
documents require Colorado authority review, and federal documents require
federal authentication review where applicable.

## Operational Rail

1. Identify required records.
2. Separate records by issuing authority.
3. Route Florida records through the appropriate Florida pathway.
4. Route Colorado records through Colorado retrieval and apostille workflow.
5. Flag translation for receiving-authority review.
6. Preserve chain of custody.
7. Return the final packet with controlled status and release confirmation.

The Milano Seal is document execution and routing infrastructure for
international use. It does not promise receiving-authority acceptance.
