# Social Authority Asset System

## Social Posting Doctrine

The Milano Seal social presence is a trust-building layer, not a sales layer. Every post should reinforce authority, specificity, and professional credibility without making claims we cannot support.

### Core Rules
- Post as an authority, not a promoter
- Reference specific document types, corridors, and workflows
- Never claim government approval, embassy verification, or guaranteed acceptance
- Never imply legal advice
- Never use urgency language that implies pressure

## Caption Copy Guidelines

- Lead with the document type or corridor
- Include the service action (notarized, apostilled, couriered, released)
- End with a quiet CTA or intake reference
- Keep captions under 150 characters for feed posts
- Use full sentences for carousel or long-form posts

## Hashtag Rules

### Approved Hashtags

### Prohibited Hashtags
- Do not use hashtags that imply guaranteed outcomes
- Do not use hashtags associated with immigration filing services
- Do not use hashtags that imply legal advice

### Volume
- Maximum 5 hashtags per post on Instagram
- Maximum 3 hashtags per post on LinkedIn
- Hashtags on X/Twitter only when directly relevant

---

## Founder Tagging SOP

- Tag founder account on milestone posts only
- Do not tag founder on every post
- Founder tags should be reserved for corridor launches, alliance announcements, and institutional partnerships
- Founder account should amplify posts with a comment, not just a like

## GBP Authority Layer

### Google Business Profile Posting Cadence
- Minimum 2 posts per week on GBP
- Post types: service update, corridor highlight, matter milestone, professional referral note
- Each GBP post should include a CTA link to themilanoseal.com/intake or the relevant corridor/service page
- Use GBP photo posts for visual trust signals (seal imagery, document imagery, execution imagery)

### GBP Review Response Protocol
- Respond to every review within 48 hours
- Response tone: professional, specific, grateful, never defensive
- Reference the service type and corridor when possible
- Never disclose client matter details in responses

### GBP Q&A Management
- Monitor Q&A weekly
- Answer all questions with accurate, non-legal-advice language
- Flag questions that require operator review before answering
