## Italian Citizenship Consultant Outreach

**Subject:** Document authentication for Italian citizenship claims — we handle the full chain.

---

Italian citizenship by descent claims require precise document work: birth records, marriage certificates, and death records from multiple generations, each needing apostille authentication before submission to an Italian Consulate. The document corridor from a U.S. county clerk to a consulate is one we know well.

The Milano Seal manages the full authentication chain for Italian citizenship document packages. We coordinate notarization where required, apostille through the appropriate state authority, and delivery to the consulate submission address your client is using. We track every step in custody.

If your clients are encountering delays or uncertainty around document authentication, we are available to take that piece off your plate entirely.

## First Client Intake Checklist (Client-Facing)

Before submitting your intake form, please have the following ready:

- The original document or a clear scan of the document you need authenticated
- The name of the country where the document will be used
- The name of the authority, institution, or organization that will receive the document (if known)
- The purpose of the document (for example: property transaction, estate filing, visa application, consulate submission)
- Your full legal name as it appears on the document
- The name of any other signers if applicable
- A valid email address where we can reach you
- Your preferred appointment type: in-person or Remote Online Notarization (if notarization is required)

If you are unsure about any of these items, submit what you have. Your coordinator will follow up to collect anything missing before your matter is opened.
