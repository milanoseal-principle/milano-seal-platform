Milano Seal UI may include notary, RON, apostille, international document execution, document coordination, real estate document execution, legal document support logistics, identity verification, execution QA, custody tracking, shipping/tracking, and quote/payment/scheduling.

Blocked or quarantined examples: Demesmin & Dover legal intake, PIP suits, provider BD, personal injury case routing, accident attorney keywords, 1PLNT/cannabis, TM Karma growth system sales copy, legal advice, and attorney representation language.

Blocked examples may appear in this document and boundary dictionaries only. They must not appear in client-facing Milano Seal UI copy.
