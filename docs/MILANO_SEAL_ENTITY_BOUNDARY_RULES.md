# Milano Seal Entity Boundary Rules

Milano Seal UI may include notary, RON, apostille, international document execution, document coordination, real estate document execution, legal document support logistics, identity verification, execution QA, custody tracking, shipping/tracking, and quote/payment/scheduling.

Blocked or quarantined examples include outside legal-intake workflows, external case-recovery systems, provider-business-development workflows, personal injury case routing, accident-attorney marketing terms, unrelated growth-system sales copy, legal advice, and attorney representation language.

Blocked examples may appear in internal boundary dictionaries only when required for enforcement. They must not appear in client-facing Milano Seal UI copy or become part of the Milano Seal canonical Matter model.
