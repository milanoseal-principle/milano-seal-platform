# Milano Seal Hague and Consular Research Protocol

Confirmed facts must come from official sources: HCCH Apostille Convention status table, U.S. Department of State authentication guidance, Florida Statutes Chapter 117, state apostille/notary pages, embassy or consular official pages, and OFAC or U.S. sanctions sources.

If official confirmation is missing, set `needsOfficialReview: true` and route the matter to operator or founder/admin review.
