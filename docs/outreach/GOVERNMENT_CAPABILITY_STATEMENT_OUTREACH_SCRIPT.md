# Government Capability Statement Outreach Script

Use this to introduce The Milano Seal™ to public-sector procurement and
department contacts alongside the capability statement. Keep every claim true.
No guarantees, no fabricated clients, no implied partnership or endorsement.

## Do / Don't
- **Do** describe capabilities factually and offer to register as a vendor.
- **Do** state that final acceptance remains subject to the receiving authority.
- **Don't** claim government approval, endorsement, or an official partnership.
- **Don't** promise a contract, award, procurement outcome, or acceptance.
- **Don't** reference clients or past performance that has not actually occurred.
- **Don't** offer legal advice.
