# The Milano Seal™ — VA Delivery SOP

**Classification:** Internal SOP  
**Module:** VA Operations — Delivery Phase  
**Status:** Active  
**Audience:** Virtual Assistant Operators

---

## VA Role in Delivery

The VA role supports the delivery phase through tracking, client follow-up, and document checklist verification. The VA does **not** control the final release.

---

## VA Delivery Checklist (Required)

For every matter entering the delivery phase, the VA must verify:

- [ ] Courier tracking number present in dashboard
- [ ] Carrier name confirmed
- [ ] Estimated delivery date logged
- [ ] Client email on file
- [ ] Document vault upload confirmed (digital delivery only)
- [ ] Founder notified that matter is ready for final release

---

## Escalation Protocol

| Trigger | VA Action |
|---|---|
| Tracking number not active 24h after courier release | Flag to founder immediately |
| Client contacts VA about delivery | Log message, notify founder within 2 hours |
| Delivery shows "exception" or "returned" | Flag to founder immediately — do not communicate outcome to client without instruction |
| Client requests document re-send | Flag to founder — VA does not re-upload or re-generate links |

---

## Communication Rules

- VA does not send final delivery confirmation emails — that is founder-handled
- VA may send check-in follow-ups only with explicit founder approval per matter
- VA does not discuss pricing, payment disputes, or apostille outcomes with clients
- All client-facing communication from VA uses the approved templates only

---

## Prohibited VA Actions — Hard Stop

- Accessing `operator_notes`, `internal_notes`, `discount_note`
- Viewing or copying Wise payment URLs
- Triggering courier release
- Confirming payment
- Sending review request link
- Marking matter as `completed`
- Accessing storage vault paths
- Editing matter payment records

Violation of any prohibited action must be reported to the founder immediately.
