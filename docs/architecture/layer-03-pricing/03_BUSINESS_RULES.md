# Business Rules

Layer-specific state authority is defined in `../../LAYER_MANIFEST.json`.

Rules:
- Truth precedes state; state precedes risk/opportunity; next action follows evidence.
- Never infer success from navigation, redirects, stale projections, or missing data.
- Protected downstream transitions remain blocked until their own prerequisites pass.
- Unknown never silently becomes ready, rejected, paid, executed, accepted, delivered, or released.
- No legal-advice or receiving-authority-acceptance guarantee is created by this layer.
