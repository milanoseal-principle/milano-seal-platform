# Final Intelligence Operator OS

## Purpose
The Final Intelligence Operator OS adds internal execution intelligence, packaging delivery controls, website alignment guards, and public copy safety checks without replacing existing production systems.

## Source Extraction
Canonical UI patterns were extracted as visual and structural concepts only: black/gold/ivory tokens, luxury card rhythm, status badge patterns, command-center structure, cinematic glow, and internal dashboard cadence.

## Accepted Concepts
Accepted concepts include internal operator modules, honest empty states, typed configuration, source-status labels, operator-review labels, receiving-authority warnings, and CSS-only cinematic polish.

## Rejected Raw Items
Rejected raw items include uploaded package replacement, fake seed data, fake prospect data, fake campaign data, fake revenue charts, public price menus, unsafe compliance claims, direct browser AI calls, and spreadsheet/database replacement.

## Upgraded Replacements
Rejected items were upgraded into internal-only dashboards, typed modules, validators, audit docs, noindex routes, no-fake-data empty states, and receiving-authority-safe copy policies.

## Routes
- `/dashboard/operator-spine`
- `/dashboard/packaging-delivery`

## Modules
The upgrade includes Operator Intelligence Spine, Packaging & Delivery Checklist Engine, Website Alignment Guard, Public Copy Safety Guard, and extraction audit documentation.

## Internal-Only Rule
The dashboard routes are internal-only and noindexed. They must not be exposed through homepage navigation, footer navigation, public sitemap strategy, SEO routes, client portal pre-login pages, or public pricing pages.

## Future Data Connection Plan
Future data connections may use existing server-side APIs only. Wise, payment confirmation, scheduling unlock, release gate, custody ledger, Supabase migrations, auth/RLS, BlueNotary internals, Shippo internals, package dependencies, and public pricing policy remain untouched.
