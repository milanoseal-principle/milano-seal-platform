## Core Visual System

| Color | Hex | Name |
|---|---|---|
| Matte Black | `#0A0A0A` | Archive Black |
| Milano Gold | `#C6A65B` | Antique Gold _(canonical)_ |
| Ivory Parchment | `#F6F1E6` | Embassy Parchment |
| Signature White | `#FFFFFF` | Clean White |

> **Gold note:** Existing production UI widely uses `#C6A55A`, a near-identical antique gold, retained as a legacy alias. New work should prefer the canonical `#C6A65B`.

## Typography

- **Primary — Trajan Pro:** display / logo lockups
- **Secondary — Cinzel:** subheads / refined caps
- **Body — Playfair Display:** body copy

## Brand Personality

Authoritative · Precise · Discreet · Elevated · Reliable

## Tone of Voice

- **Tone:** Calm, confident, professional, refined
- **Style:** Direct, clear, solution-focused
- **Do:** Use legal clarity and premium language
- **Don't:** Use casual language or industry slang
- **Voice example:** _"We coordinate your documents with precision so you can move forward with confidence."_

## Tagline Usage

Always all-caps, letter-spaced, gold or white on dark background. **Never alter the wording.**
