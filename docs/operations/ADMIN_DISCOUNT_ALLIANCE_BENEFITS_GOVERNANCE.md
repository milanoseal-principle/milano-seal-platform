# Governance lock

## Discount administration

Founder/admin may manage discount policy objects only. The admin manager is not authorized to write Matter monetary fields, mark payment confirmed, unlock scheduling, or unlock execution.

## Alliance benefit presentation

Alliance members receive read-only visibility into benefits explicitly assigned to their authenticated partner account. Benefit administration remains founder/admin only.

## Referral attribution

Authenticated Alliance referral submissions carry the partner referral code/email into the existing referral endpoint. Referral attribution resolves to a partner ID before staging.

## Revenue reporting

Discount reporting may describe redeemed Matter value and courtesy value. It must not infer a conversion rate without an issued/exposure denominator and must not label adjusted Matter value as paid revenue. Wise reconciliation is the payment/revenue authority.
