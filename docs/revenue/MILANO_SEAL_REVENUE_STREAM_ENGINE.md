# Milano Seal Revenue Stream Engine

The Milano Seal is Secure International Document Execution & Authentication Infrastructure. The revenue stream engine models how the company monetizes coordination around Secure Records Intake, RON eligibility review, apostille/authentication routing, signer coordination, partner dispatch, International Courier Release, and secure execution tracking.

## Revenue Lanes

1. Intake / eligibility review
2. RON coordination
3. Mobile / airport-area execution
4. Apostille / authentication coordination
5. Translation coordination
6. Courier / scan-back / custody
7. Founder / executive review
8. Attorney / professional coordinator lane
9. RON Alliance partner spread
10. Repeat client / family desk
11. Airport-area emergency execution

## Safety Rules

- No fake revenue.
- No guaranteed acceptance.
- No guaranteed partner assignments.
- No guaranteed income.
- No legal, immigration, custody, or travel admissibility advice.
- No TSA clearance claim.
- No airport approved claim.
- No airline approval claim.
- Internal partner margin data must not be displayed publicly.
- Wise, payment confirmation, release gate, custody ledger, Supabase RLS/auth, Shippo, and BlueNotary logic are protected and must not be changed by this engine.
