# Milano Seal Global Acceptance Intelligence

Milano Seal provides execution coordination and routing intelligence. Acceptance always depends on the receiving authority, document type, jurisdiction, language requirements, and current government/consular rules.

This layer supports Hague vs non-Hague routing, state apostille, federal authentication, embassy or consular legalization, RON acceptance review, wet-ink recommendation, certified translation review, international shipping/custody review, sanctions or restricted destination review, founder/admin review, alliance member restrictions, and safe client/operator/founder summaries.

It does not provide a government opinion, foreign authority acceptance guarantee, RON acceptance guarantee, embassy legal opinion, or public secret-sauce disclosure.
