# Government & Public Sector Vertical — Investor Insert

## Thesis
Public-sector entities generate a continuous, recurring need for notarized,
witnessed, and authenticated documents — contracts, board resolutions, records,
emergency-response paperwork, and vendor documentation. These buyers procure
through structured, repeatable channels (vendor registration + solicitations),
which rewards a provider that is registered, responsive, and compliance-first.

## Why The Milano Seal™ fits
- Existing payment-gated, custody-aware execution engine reused unchanged.
- Florida notary commission (#668288) and RON authorization already in place.
- A dedicated CRM, procurement tracker, vendor-registration tracker, and
  bid/no-bid scoring purpose-built for the public-sector motion.

## Motion
1. Register as a vendor across a priority list of Tampa Bay-area entities
   (Pinellas, Hillsborough, Pasco, St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Pinellas County
   Schools, and more).
2. Monitor solicitations against selected commodity categories.
3. Score and pursue opportunities with a disciplined bid/no-bid process.
4. Convert one-time engagements into on-call, task-order, and annual-contract
   revenue streams.

## Revenue model
One-time engagements, master service agreements, blanket purchase agreements,
annual contracts, emergency contracts, on-call services, task orders, and
department retainers.

## Honest disclosures
- This is an early-stage, build-out vertical. There are no claimed existing
  agency clients, awards, or past performance.
- Vendor registration and solicitation participation do not guarantee contracts,
  procurement outcomes, awards, or acceptance.
- The Milano Seal™ does not provide legal advice or claim government approval,
  endorsement, or official partnership. Final acceptance remains subject to the
  receiving authority.

## Metrics we will track
registered_agencies, open_opportunities, submitted_bids, award_rate,
contract_value, pipeline_value, renewal_value, average_procurement_cycle,
government_revenue, government_clients.
