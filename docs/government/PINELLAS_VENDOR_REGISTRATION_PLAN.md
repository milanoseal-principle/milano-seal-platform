# Pinellas Vendor Registration Plan

A concrete, replicable plan to register The Milano Seal™ (Milano Seal
International LLC) as a vendor with Pinellas-area public entities and to begin
tracking solicitations. Registration status is only ever marked "active" once a
verified vendor ID is actually issued — never assumed.

## 8-Step Plan

1. **Register in the OpenGov vendor portal.** Create the Milano Seal
   International LLC vendor profile in the county's OpenGov procurement portal.
2. **Complete the W-9.** Provide a current, signed IRS Form W-9 with the correct
   legal name, entity type, and TIN.
3. **Prepare the capability statement.** Finalize a one-page capability statement
   (core competencies, differentiators, NAICS codes, company data). No
   fabricated past performance.
4. **Add E&O / commission / RON authorization documents.** Upload errors &
   omissions insurance, the Florida notary commission (#668288), and RON
   authorization documentation.
5. **Select relevant commodity categories.** Choose the applicable NIGP/NAICS
   commodity categories (e.g. notary services, document support). Verify each
   code against the portal before selecting.
6. **Monitor solicitations.** Subscribe to bid/RFP/RFQ notifications for the
   selected categories and review postings on a regular cadence.
7. **Track opportunities in the dashboard.** Record each solicitation and its
   stage in `/dashboard/government` (CRM board + opportunity board).
8. **Replicate with additional entities.** Repeat steps 1–7 with Hillsborough,
   Pasco, St. Petersburg, Clearwater, and Pinellas County Schools.

## Notes
- Being a registered vendor does not guarantee a contract, procurement outcome,
  or award.
- Portals differ; commodity code systems and versions vary — always verify.
- Keep insurance, commission, and RON documentation current to avoid expired
  registration status.
